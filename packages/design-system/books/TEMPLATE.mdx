Open with what this is and who needs it, in two or three sentences. State the
decision the reader arrives with, and answer it here rather than at the end.

## A heading that states the outcome

Not "Overview" and not a noun phrase — "Form controls inherit their type", "The
grid is the only literal length". A reader scanning headings should be able to
learn the rules without reading the paragraphs.

Prose explains why the system is shaped this way and what the rule prevents. Values
belong in a table:

| Token | Value | Role |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| `--cn-grid` | `0.5rem` | The atomic unit of alignment. |

## A heading with live source under it

<SomeSpecimen />

Use a specimen where the page would otherwise repeat a stylesheet by hand. Say in
one line what the reader is looking at; do not narrate what the specimen already
shows.

## What this does not cover

Name the neighbouring things this book deliberately leaves alone and who owns them.
A reader who came for one of them should leave knowing where to look, and a future
agent should not read the omission as an oversight.
