What it is, and the one mechanism a reader cannot derive from the invocation below:
what resolves when, what a value inherits, what it costs at build or render time.
Two or three sentences. Do not restate the component's name.

## When to use it

- The case it is for, as an instruction rather than a description.
- The case it is for.

Close on the case it is not for, naming the alternative and linking its book, so a
reader who came to the wrong page leaves knowing where to go. State what the
component does not do when a reader would otherwise assume it — a progress
indicator that cannot report progress says so here.
