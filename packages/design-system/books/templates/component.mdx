What the component is for, as an instruction: "Use a <thing> to <do something for a
reader>". One or two sentences. The `description` above already states what it is, so
a definition here is a restatement — write the purpose instead.

## When to use it

Where a component is one presentation of a capability, say so and link that
capability's book. Its placement, its container rules and its states live there, and
a reader follows one link rather than reading them twice.

State only what this component adds: the case it is for, as an instruction, and the
case it is not for, naming the alternative and linking its book.
