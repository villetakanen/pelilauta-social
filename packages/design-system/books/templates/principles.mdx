Open with what this subject does for the product and the means we chose, in two or
three first-person-plural sentences. "We rely on two complementary typefaces: Lato
provides an approachable, highly readable voice, while Roboto Mono brings structure
and precision to code and metadata."

## A Topic, Named Plainly

Headings are Title Case topic names — "Type Scale & Vertical Rhythm" — optionally
carrying their qualities: "Lato: Warmth, Clarity, and Universal Scale". A heading is
not a slogan and not a sentence.

Every choice carries its rationale, comparative where alternatives were live and
quantified where numbers exist:

* A Labeled Quality: What the choice provides, tied to this product's real surfaces.
* Another Quality: With the numbers — "because 1.414² = 2, every two steps doubles
  the size (17px → 24px → 34px)".

### A Subsection For One Decision

A `##` topic holds its decisions as `###` subsections rather than spawning sibling
