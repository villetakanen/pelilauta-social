Open with what this is and who needs it, in two or three sentences. State the

## A heading that states the outcome

Not "Overview" and not a noun phrase — "Form controls inherit their type", "The
grid is the only literal length". A reader scanning headings should be able to
learn the rules without reading the paragraphs.

Prose explains how to choose and what goes wrong. Values belong in a table:

| Token | Value | Role |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| `--cn-grid` | `0.5rem` | The atomic unit of alignment. |
