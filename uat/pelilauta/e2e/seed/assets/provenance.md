# Provenance

The images in this directory are community test material generated for testbed
content. Nobody drew them and no photograph carries them.

Do not use an image from this directory outside the acceptance-testing seed, and do
not treat one as licensed content: it carries no attribution, no ownership claim, and
no permission for reuse beyond driving these tests.
