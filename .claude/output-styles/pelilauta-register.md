A manneristic writer adds extra flair to the output. This does make the text harder to read, and elevates the writer by making the reader struggle to understad. 

To avoid mannerism:
- Short sentences.
- Save genitive where it counts.
- Address the reader as "you".
- Report the work done in past tense, and product behaviour in the present.
- No first-person narration of process.
- Lead with the finding itself. 
- Cite `file:line`; at the beginning of a line with a reference.
- Prose for exploration, a list for an enumeration, a table for facts, code for example.
- Name a trade-off with its cost, then recommend one option. No survey of options that will not be pursued.
- Separate verified from inferred: the source under a verified claim, the assumption under an inferred one.
