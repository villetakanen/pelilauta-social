`CnAvatar` renders the pictorial identity mark: a profile's image, its initials, or
the generic glyph. It renders on the server, and its fallback needs no script: the
initials render beneath the image, and a failed image load paints nothing over
them. The backdrop behind the initials is derived from `nick` at render time, so it
appears in no stylesheet and cannot be set by a consumer.

## When to use it

Render `CnAvatar` wherever a profile appears as a picture. Pass `nick` whenever it is
known, including alongside `src` — it supplies the initials and the backdrop the mark
falls back to, and the accessible name.

Use the textual mark, `.cn-nick`, where the profile appears as a name instead. Both
marks, when to reach for which, and what an anchor does to either are in
[Identity Mark](/base/identity-mark), which is also where the states below are shown
in Light and Dark.

The component does not name a missing nick. What the absence means — a deleted
profile, an anonymous one, a redacted one — and the localised word for it are the
application's; it prints the word in its own text while the mark stays the generic
glyph.

## Reference

### Props

| Prop | Type | Purpose |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| `src` | `string` | The profile's image. Falls back to the initials, or to the glyph, when absent or unloadable. |
| `nick` | `string` | The profile's name. Supplies the initials, the backdrop, and the accessible name. Absent, the mark is anonymous. |
| `size` | `'small' \| 'medium'` | One and a half lines, or two. Defaults to `'medium'`. |
| `aria-hidden` | `boolean` | Hides the mark from assistive technology. Defaults to `false`. |

### Tokens

The component declares no colour of its own. It consumes the identity roles —
`--cn-avatar-backdrop-from`, `--cn-avatar-backdrop-to` and `--cn-on-avatar` — which
[Identity Mark](/base/identity-mark) publishes with the contrast they guarantee.
Geometry comes from `--cn-line`, and elevation from `--cn-shadow-elevation-1`.

## Accessibility

The mark announces `nick` as its accessible name, and nothing else: the design
system publishes no wording around it, because it serves three languages and
translates none of them.

Pass `aria-hidden` wherever the composition already names the profile — a linked
avatar whose anchor carries the nick, or a mark beside the textual one. A mark with
no `nick` is always hidden, since it has no name to announce.

## Not shown here

**Initials under an image with transparent regions.** Inside a hydrated island the
component hides the initials once the image reports a successful load, so they
cannot show through the image's transparency. Server-rendered without hydration,
the initials stay painted beneath the image. The behaviour needs hydration this
site does not build, so the pages here render only the scriptless layering.
