`CnAppBar` renders the application's `banner` landmark: a leading region, a title, then
the actions a consumer supplies through its default slot. It states no target, state
surface or CnIcon size of its own — every action placed in it is a chrome action, compact
by declaration. [Chrome Actions](/base/chrome-actions) specifies that geometry.

## When to use it

- Mount it at the block start of application chrome. The chrome container decides where
  it sits and what cedes space to it; the bar does not place itself.
- Pass `noun` naming the page's context. Omit it, and the leading region carries the
  product's logomark instead.
- Pass `href` naming where the identity leads — the root of whatever the bar names. The
  logomark and the wordmark become one link to it.
- Pass `modal` when the bar fronts a dialog, or a page standing in for one.

It carries no navigation of its own beyond the back action a modal bar places, and
redefines no chrome-action geometry — mount [Chrome Actions](/base/chrome-actions)
helpers, or your own actions built to that same contract, in its slot.

## Variants and states

Two axes vary: what the leading region carries, and the modal variant that replaces it
along with the actions.

### Leading region

<DsComposition caption="The default bar, with no context noun, standing for the site, in Light and Dark">
  <CnAppBarSpecimens group="no-noun" />
</DsComposition>

Passing no `noun` renders the product's logomark, rather than no leading region at all.

#### Below the small breakpoint

No rail holds a navigation trigger there, so the bar reserves `8 × --cn-grid` at its
start edge for it and shows no context glyph in that space. The title falls back to
`shortTitle`, where one is passed.

<DsComposition caption="The same bar above and below --cn-breakpoint-small">

</DsComposition>

### Modal

<DsComposition caption="The modal bar, in Light and Dark">
  <CnAppBarSpecimens group="modal" />
</DsComposition>

The leading region carries the back action in place of a context glyph or the site
mark, and the bar renders no actions — a modal bar's slot content is dropped rather
than hidden. It rests on an opaque surface at elevation 2 and carries no veil.

## Composition

Placement, and what cedes space to the bar, belong to the application chrome
container. Every element passed to its slot is a chrome action, and the bar declares
the compact presentation for them; a labelled action inside it still renders compact.

## Reference

### Props

| Prop | Type | Purpose |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| `title` | `string` | Required. The page's title, truncated rather than wrapped. |
| `shortTitle` | `string` | Shown in place of `title` below `--cn-breakpoint-small`. |
| `noun` | `string` | Context glyph. Absent renders the product's logomark. |
| `modal` | `boolean` | A modal bar: the back action replaces the leading region, and actions are dropped. Defaults to `false`. |
| `backLabel` | `string` | Accessible name for the back control. Localise it; passed through to `CnBackAction`. |
| `href` | `string` | Where the identity leads. Absent leaves the identity inert. |

### Tokens

`CnAppBar` declares no custom properties of its own. Its geometry reads `--cn-grid` and
`--cn-gap`, its colour `--cn-color-surface` and `--cn-color-on-surface`, its modal elevation
`--cn-shadow-elevation-2`, its title type step the h3 tokens the document outline does
not use, and its actions' presentation [Chrome Actions](/base/chrome-actions).

## Accessibility

The bar's `<header>` carries the implicit `banner` role only where the surrounding
chrome does not nest it inside sectioning content — an application places it directly,
never inside `<main>`, `<article>`, `<aside>`, `<nav>` or `<section>`.

The title renders as a `<p>`, not a heading: the document's outline is the page's, and
the bar is chrome. A context noun's CnIcon is decorative and exposes nothing; the
logomark instead carries `role="img"` and `aria-label="Pelilauta.social"`.
A modal bar's back action takes its accessible name from `backLabel`, which a consumer
must localise — its own default is a placeholder, not a name.

## Not shown here

**The veil.** The bar paints nothing while the page is at its top; a veil fades in,
replacing that absent background rather than accompanying it, as content passes beneath
on scroll. The mechanism reads document scroll through a scroll-driven animation, which
a specimen on a page this short cannot produce. Verified at rest, and once scrolled, by
`apps/design/e2e/cn-app-bar.spec.ts`.
