Use a reaction button to let a reader toggle their love reaction on an entry, and to
show every reader how many there are.

## When to use it

Use it in the compact action region of an entry or a reply, wherever the reader may
love the thing and see the count. Where the reaction is shown but not offered — a
reader who is not signed in, an entry that cannot be loved — render it `disabled`;
the count still reads.

It is a toggle on the content beside it, not a command of its own. An action that
does something once — send, share, delete — is a [button](/base/buttons).

## Example

The consumer supplies the state. Pass the current `count`, `countLabel` and
`pressed`, receive the native click, and supply the updated values once the change
is real; the component keeps showing what it was given.

<Composition caption="A consumer receiving the click and supplying the new count and pressed state">
  <ReactionButtonDemo client:load />
</Composition>

```svelte
<script>
  import CnReactionButton from '@design-system/components/CnReactionButton.svelte'
</script>

<CnReactionButton
  label={t('threads:reaction.love')}
  count={entry.loveCount}
  countLabel={t('threads:reaction.loveCount', { count: entry.loveCount })}
  pressed={entry.lovedByReader}
  onclick={() => toggleLove(entry)}
/>
```

The component is an island: without `client:load`, or the framework's equivalent,
the control renders its supplied state and the click reaches no one.

A supplied flip of `pressed` replays a burst out of the state surface, coloured by
the state it lands. The click alone plays nothing, so a change that fails to persist
celebrates nothing.

Two presentations ship. The default is an entry's; `small` is a reply's.

## Reference

### Props

| Prop | Type | Purpose |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| `label` | `string` | Required. The toggle's accessible name. Localise it. |
| `count` | `number` | Required. The reaction count, a non-negative integer, rendered as given. |
| `countLabel` | `string` | Required. The count's accessible description. Localise it, and change it with `count`. |
| `pressed` | `boolean` | Whether the current reader's reaction is among the counted. |
| `disabled` | `boolean` | Renders the control inert: no pointer, no focus, no click. The supplied state and count stay visible. |
| `small` | `boolean` | The compact presentation. |
| `onclick` | `(event) => void` | Required. Runs on every activation, with the button as the event's `currentTarget`. |

## Accessibility

The control is one native toggle button: one focus stop, activated anywhere on its
surface or count, announcing `label` as its name, the pressed state through
`aria-pressed`, and `countLabel` as its description. Activation, the keyboard, focus
and the disabled state are the platform's.

The heart is decoration and reaches no reader, so the name and the count's meaning
are the consumer's to supply — the visible count is a bare numeral, and `countLabel`
is what says what it counts.
