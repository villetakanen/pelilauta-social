## Type

A field is set in the mono family, which `packages/design-system/styles/fonts.css`
decides. It states its own size and leading rather than inheriting them, so a
field in a caption is the same field as one in prose.

The size is `1.0186rem`, 16.3px at the default root size. Roboto Mono carries a
taller lowercase than Lato — an x-height of 0.5283 of its em against 0.5065 — so
mono at the 17px reading step would stand about 4% taller than the prose beside
it. At 16.3px the two lowercases stand equal, which is the height a reader
measures a line by.

## Spacing

A field spaces its content two grid units on every side, and the reading leading
stands between them. The block padding is three units in total and each line is
three, so a field lands on the grid at every line count. A field draws no inner
control, so the box a reader sees is the box a reader taps.

| | value | grid units |
| :--- | ---: | ---: |
| Padding, block | 12px | 1.5 |
| Padding, inline | 16px | 2 |
| Leading | 24px | 3 |
| Height, one line | 48px | 6 |
| Height, two lines | 72px | 9 |
| Height, three lines | 96px | 12 |
| Margin, block | 4px | 0.5 |
| Row, one line | 56px | 7 |

The field a reader sees is six units tall, and half a unit of margin on either
side puts it in a seven-unit row — the same visual-inside-physical pair a button
takes.

A label wrapping a field carries the container instead, and the field inside it
keeps only its type. The container stands the same 48px: half a unit of padding,
the label's two units, the value's three, half a unit of padding.

| | value | grid units |
| :--- | ---: | ---: |
| Padding, block | 4px | 0.5 |
| Padding, inline | 16px | 2 |
| Label line | 16px | 2 |
| Value leading | 24px | 3 |
| Height, one line | 48px | 6 |
| Height, two lines | 72px | 9 |

## Hover and focus

A field distinguishes three states — rest, hover and focus. Hover moves the
indicator alone: the fill stays where it rests, so a pointer crossing a form
does not wash its fields in and out, and the indicator takes a high-visibility
step of the primary family and grows from one eighth of a grid unit to two, and
the label crosses to the primary scale at the step it rests on. Focus moves both. Each needs a pointer or a
keyboard to reach: hover the field below with a pointer, then tab to it or tap
into it, in both colour schemes.

Focus keeps the width hover gave the indicator and darkens it — primary 40
against hover's primary 50, so the line changes in lightness and not in hue
alone, and a reader who cannot separate the two hues still sees the state. The
fill changes to the primary family with it, and the label moves to the primary
scale. The selector is
`:focus-within`, held on the control and on a label wrapping it, because
`:focus-visible` does not fire for a reader who reaches the field by touch.

A field draws no focus ring, and the active indicator is the focus indication.
A focused text control matches `:focus-visible` whichever way the reader
reached it, because typing is expected of it, so a ring written for the keyboard
lands on every click as well. Material Design 3's filled field takes the same
position: its focus state is a second active indicator cross-faded over the
resting one, and no ring.

That meets WCAG 2.2 SC 2.4.7, which asks that focus be visible rather than that
it be an outline. SC 2.4.13 Focus Appearance, which would ask for the ring's
geometry, is AAA, and a filled field does not target it.

A disabled field answers no pointer: hover does not reach it.

`--cn-color-field-disabled` is not settled. `specs/design-system/fields/spec.md`
reserves the role and leaves its value for this page to settle by experiment. The
alternative not taken here is the Material Design 3 shape: a fill that collapses
toward the surface, with the active indicator surviving as the only trace of the
field. Judge the fill above against that alternative before the value ships.

## A labelled field

A field's accessible name comes from a `<label>` wrapping the control. The label
becomes the filled container and the field inside it draws nothing of its own,
so the name stands inside the fill, above the reader's words — Material Design
3's populated filled field. The label text is the one part of a field set in
Lato, at the label step: it is the field's name, written for a reader, where the
value below it is the reader's text.

The label is also the one part of a field that states a foreground colour: a
surface step in from the end of the scale, off the near-black the body takes in
Light and off the near-white it takes in Dark. The surface steps are saturated at
hue 242, so the name carries a hint of colour where the value keeps the body
foreground — the system's word beside the reader's.
