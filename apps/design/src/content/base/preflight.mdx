Preflight establishes a global style baseline across browsers. Applications receive the
reset through the design system CSS entry point before presentation styles. Importing
tokens alone does not apply the reset.

[Andy Bell's modern reset](https://piccalil.li/blog/a-more-modern-css-reset/) and
[Tailwind Preflight](https://tailwindcss.com/docs/preflight) inform the reset.

## Reset and presentation

Preflight removes browser defaults and normalises inconsistencies shared across surfaces.
A global selector alone does not make a presentation rule part of the reset.

[Fields](/base/fields) and [Links, actions and buttons](/base/links-actions-buttons) define
field presentation, form spacing, sizing variants and interaction treatments.
[Fonts](/base/fonts) supplies font families. [Typography](/tokens/typography) supplies
type treatment. Scrollbars retain browser presentation.

## Document composition

Apply [Content Area](/utilities/content-area) to authored prose that needs document
spacing, list gutters and media constraints. Scoped treatments and nested components
replace reset presentation through the cascade without specificity escalation.

## Sizing and spacing

Elements and before and after pseudo-elements use border-box sizing. Preflight
removes margins and padding from elements, before and after pseudo-elements, backdrops
and file-selector buttons. Layout and content treatments supply spacing.

## List markers

Authored ordered and unordered lists retain native markers. Add `role="list"` to
an interface list to remove its markers. The explicit role preserves list
semantics for assistive technology. The reset removes list margins and padding in both
cases.

## Controls

Preflight makes buttons, inputs, selects and textareas inherit font family, size and line
height from their container. Later font and control treatments can replace that baseline.
Unstyled controls retain native rendering, keyboard operation and visible keyboard focus.

Textareas without a `rows` attribute receive a minimum height for multiline entry.
Textareas resize vertically to preserve the inline limits of the containing layout.

## Hidden content

The reset keeps elements with `hidden` outside layout. The reset leaves `hidden="until-found"`
available for find-in-page to reveal in browsers that support that state.

## Media

Preflight leaves media display and sizing to the browser. Apply media constraints from the
containing capability when images or video must fit an offered width.

## Document colours

The body fills at least the dynamic viewport height and applies theme background and
foreground colours, including on pages without a containing surface.
[Surface](/utilities/surface) defines surface roles and treatments above that baseline.

## Hydration wrappers

Astro islands add no layout box. Authored island children participate in the surrounding
flex or grid layout before and after hydration as though the wrapper were absent.
