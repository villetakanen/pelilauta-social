## Spatial grid

Three base measurements govern component alignment, internal padding, and vertical rhythm:
- `--cn-grid` (0.5rem / 8px): Base atomic unit.
- `--cn-gap` (1rem / 16px): Standard gap and surface inset (2x grid).
- `--cn-line` (1.5rem / 24px): Standard line height and block spacing (3x grid).

## Elevation shadows

The four elevation shadows are declared in `styles/color-theme.css`, not here, but they are
grid multiples and depend on `--cn-grid`. `styles/tokens.css` composes both families; this
section states that dependency. Their values are in [Colour](/tokens/color).
