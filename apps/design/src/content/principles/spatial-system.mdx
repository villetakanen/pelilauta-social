Pelilauta's spatial system starts from an 8px base unit. It defines recurring
spacing, radius, and shadow measurements as well as many component dimensions.
Icon sizes are an independent `rem` scale documented in
[Iconography](/principles/iconography).

## Core Measurements

Three named measurements recur throughout the system. `--cn-grid` is the 0.5rem
base unit and a frequent compact interval. `--cn-gap` doubles it for standard
component and layout gaps. `--cn-line` triples it for body line height, minimum
row height, and separation between stacked blocks.

<SpatialMeasurements source={unitsSource} />

These are common uses rather than an exhaustive mapping between content
relationships and measurements. Components use other grid multiples where their
geometry requires them.

For the declarations and complete token families, see
[Units and grid](/tokens/units).

## Relationships in a Component

In the discussion preview, the default `--cn-gap` interval separates the avatar
from its identity text. `--cn-grid` separates the title from its metadata.
`--cn-gap` also provides the surface inset and separates the actions, while
`--cn-line` separates the identity from the message body. A component can vary
these measurements where its composition requires a different interval.

## Composition Boundaries

Components define their internal spacing. A containing grid or flex layout
usually sets the interval between sibling components with `gap`, allowing items
to reorder or wrap. A component can include exterior space when that space forms
part of its defined physical footprint.

The [Surface](/base/surface) container includes `--cn-gap` inset on every side, so
cards and panels do not require inset at the point of use. Page-level measure,
breakouts, and centred regions are defined by
[Content containers](/base/content-containers).

## Vertical Rhythm

`--cn-line` provides the 24px body line height, minimum row height, and separation
between stacked blocks. A control one line unit high aligns with adjacent body
text. Separating blocks by the same unit places the following text back on that
rhythm.

[Typography](/principles/typography) defines the body line height and documents
how the type scale adapts to its container.

### Browser Font Settings

Spatial values and breakpoint thresholds use `rem`. The design system does not
set `font-size` on `html` or `:root`. Spacing, radii, line rhythm, and layout
thresholds therefore respond to the reader's browser font setting.

</div>

At larger browser font settings, components may wrap or recompose around their
content.

## Radius and Shadow Geometry

Radius steps are one-half, one, two, and four grid units. A rounded content
container includes at least one grid unit of inset between its content and edge.

<SpatialRadii source={unitsSource} utilitySource={spatialSource} />

The unsuffixed `--cn-border-radius` token resolves to the medium step.
Despite its unsuffixed name, `.border-radius` applies the large step. The complete
radius family is in [Units and grid](/tokens/units).

Shadow offsets and blur also derive from the grid. Elevation meaning, theme
colours, and nested-surface behaviour are defined in
[Colour and Surface](/principles/color-system).
