We rely on two complementary typeface families: **Lato** provides an approachable,
high-legibility voice for interface controls, headings, and discussion content, while
**Roboto Mono** provides structure, alignment, and disambiguation for code, technical
metadata, and tabular figures.

## Type scale and vertical rhythm

Our typographic system anchors on a 17px body baseline (`--cn-font-size-text`) at regular
weight 400. All text elements align to the 8px spatial grid, and a baseline line height of
24px (`--cn-line`, 3x grid) establishes vertical rhythm across paragraphs
and control heights.

### Modular scale

To establish proportional relationships between heading tiers, we apply an Augmented
Fourth modular multiplier (1:√2, about 1:1.414).

The Augmented Fourth scale delivers clear tier distinction without consuming excessive
vertical space on compact viewports:
- **Predictable doubling:** Because 1.414² = 2, font size doubles every two steps: 17px text → 24px h4 → 34px h3 → 48px h2 → 68px h1.
- **Distinct hierarchy:** Headings achieve clear visual prominence without requiring heavy font weight overrides.
- **Mathematical harmony:** Heading steps maintain exact proportional alignment with the 24px baseline line height.

## Container-responsive downscaling

Over half of our heading instances appear within cards, preview feeds, and sidebar panels.
To prevent large headings from overwhelming compact components, heading tiers automatically
downscale by one modular step when their parent container is narrower than the small
breakpoint (`38.75rem`).

- **Container-relative evaluation:** Headings downscale via CSS Container Queries (`@container`), rather than the viewport width.
- **Proportional consistency:** Downscaling by a single modular step preserves the 1:1.414 ratio between heading levels while reducing line wrapping in narrow containers.

## Emphasis

Text emphasis can be low, default, or high. It is independent of the step: any step
takes any emphasis.

<p>Default emphasis, which needs no class.</p>
<p class="text-high">High emphasis, for the line that carries the page.</p>
