Pelilauta ships two themes, Dark and Light. Both ship in the same release.

Dark is the default and the theme the interface is designed in. Light is checked before a
change merges. Where the two disagree, Dark is kept and Light is adjusted.

The browser or operating system selects the theme through `color-scheme`. A signed-in
account may pin one theme; `apps/pelilauta` applies that at SSR. The design system reads
`color-scheme` only and never reads account state.

## Roles

The design system declares about forty roles. Each resolves to one colour in Dark and one
in Light. Components read roles. A literal colour has a single value and cannot follow the
theme, so components do not state one.

The two colour columns are not mirror images. Three cases to know:

- `--cn-surface-1` to `--cn-surface-3` are all white in Light. In Dark they are separate
  steps.
- `--cn-surface-4` is darker than the surface beneath it in Light, and lighter in Dark.
- `--cn-link` comes from the primary scale in Light and the surface scale in Dark.

## The scales

Six families. A step number is its perceived lightness: `surface-50` is `oklch(0.5 …)`.
Step 40 in one family is as light as step 40 in another. Chroma peaks in the middle of a
ramp and falls to zero at both ends, so step 0 and step 100 are neutral in every family.

<ColorRamp source={referenceSource} family="surface" intent="Hue 242. Backgrounds, elevation, borders, most text." />
<ColorRamp source={referenceSource} family="primary" intent="Hue 185 at the dark end to 110 at the light end. Links, focus, the FAB, and success." />

Primary is the only family whose hue changes along the ramp. The others hold one hue.

Steps 50 and 70 carry most of primary's work: `--cn-link` and `--cn-button-light` use 50,
and `--cn-fab` and `--cn-color-primary` use 70. Steps 80 to 99 are used by two roles.

<ColorRamp source={referenceSource} family="error" intent="Hue 318. Destructive actions and validation failures." />
<ColorRamp source={referenceSource} family="warning" intent="Hue 65. Caution." />
<ColorRamp source={referenceSource} family="info" intent="Hue 170. System feedback and state changes." />
<ColorRamp source={referenceSource} family="love" intent="Hue 30. Likes and favourites." />

These four families have four steps, not thirteen. There is no `error-30`.

Success has no family of its own. `--cn-color-success` resolves to `primary-60`, so success
and the brand share a hue.

### Choosing a step directly

Components read roles, so this applies to the design system itself. The gap between two
steps in one family puts a floor under their contrast, because the step is the lightness.
Measured across every pair in the palette:

| Step gap | Guarantees |
| :--- | :--- |
| 45 | AA Large, 3:1 |
| 60 | AA, 4.5:1 |
| 70 | AAA, 7:1 |

A gap of 40 guarantees nothing. Its worst pair is 2.36:1.

## Not settled

- What elevation means, and whether five surface roles are all needed. Three are identical
  in Light.
- Whether contrast below AA is ever acceptable on a raised Dark surface. `--cn-on-surface`
  on `--cn-surface-4` measures 4.32:1.
- Whether colour may carry meaning without an icon or label.
- Whether success sharing the primary hue is intended.
