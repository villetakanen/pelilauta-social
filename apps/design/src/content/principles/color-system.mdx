Build with **roles**, not steps. A role carries both modes; a step carries one. The scales
below are what the roles are made of — worth knowing, not worth reaching into.

## The six scales

A step number is its perceived lightness: `surface-50` is `oklch(0.5 …)`, and step 40 in
any family is as light as step 40 in any other. Chroma peaks mid-ramp and falls to zero at
both ends, which is why 0 and 100 are neutral everywhere.

<ColorRamp source={referenceSource} family="surface" intent="Cerulean 242°, held at every step. Backgrounds, elevation, borders, most text." />
<ColorRamp source={referenceSource} family="primary" intent="The brand ramp. Rotates 185° → 110° as it lightens: deep teal at the bottom, acid neon at the top." />

Primary is the only scale that changes hue as it climbs, so its dark end and its light end
are different colours, not one colour at two brightnesses. Every other family holds a
single hue.

<ColorRamp source={referenceSource} family="error" intent="318° — destructive actions and validation failures." />
<ColorRamp source={referenceSource} family="warning" intent="65° — high-visibility caution, nothing lost yet." />
<ColorRamp source={referenceSource} family="info" intent="170° — neutral system feedback and state changes." />
<ColorRamp source={referenceSource} family="love" intent="30° — likes, favourites, social approval. Not an error, however red." />

The four functional scales are four steps deep, not thirteen. There is no `error-30`.

Read the two colour columns against each other: Dark is **not** the palette flipped.
Surfaces 1–3 are plain white in Light and only separate in Dark. `--cn-surface-4` goes
*darker* than what it sits on in Light, and lighter in Dark. `--cn-link` is a brand teal in
Light and a pale blue in Dark — a different family, not a brighter version of the same one.

That is why you cannot pick a step and expect it to behave. The role has already made these
decisions.

## Where the scale runs out

Raised Dark surfaces climb towards mid-Cerulean, spending the headroom that de-emphasised
text needs. By elevation 4 there is none left:

  background="--cn-surface-4"
  modes={['dark']}

**On elevation 4, use `--cn-text` or `--cn-text-high`. Nothing else.** Even
`--cn-on-surface` — the ordinary body role everywhere else — only reaches AA Large here.

In Light both lines read fine, which is why this has to be stated rather than noticed. In
practice: a card that raises itself to a dialog cannot keep the byline treatment it used at
rest.

## When you do have to pick a step

Rarely, and then by distance. Because the step is the lightness, the gap between two steps
in one family puts a floor under their contrast — measured across every pair in the
palette:

| Step gap | Guarantees |
| :--- | :--- |
| 45 | AA Large, 3:1 |
| **60** | **AA, 4.5:1** |
| 70 | AAA, 7:1 |

A gap of 40 guarantees nothing — its worst pair is 2.36:1 — which is the trap, because 40
looks like most of a scale.

## Don't

- **Don't override a role locally.** Setting `color` to a step fixes one mode and breaks
  the other.
- **Don't use brand colour for destructive actions.** That is `error`.
- **Don't reach for `--cn-text-low` on a raised Dark surface.** See above.
- **Don't mix in `hsl`.** Derived colours use `color-mix(in oklch, …)`, or the perceptual
  spacing that makes step numbers meaningful is lost.

## What this does not cover

Values are in the [Colour](/tokens/color) lexicon, and the rules above are asserted in
`test/color-palette.test.ts` and `test/color-contrast.test.ts`. The palette is not
themeable at runtime — Cyan 4 allowed swapping the ramps and v21 has no consumer for it.
The per-account theme preference in `apps/pelilauta` is application state, outside this
system.
