# Design

The Design Vision and high level guidance for Pelilauta v21 and it's design system.

## Authority

The v20 Design System established the aesthetic we are now porting to the v18 business logic.
Where that authority is lacking, human supervision is needed for decision.

## Voice

The design system speaks as "we" and publishes every decision with its reasons.
The register is professional design-domain writing; the exemplar is the published
Typography principles book at `/principles/typography`.
